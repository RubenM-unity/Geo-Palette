Every place has a colour.

Drop a pin anywhere on Earth. Geo·Palette samples a square kilometre of satellite imagery, distils it into a palette, then digs up the dishes, the people and the photographs that belong to that exact spot.

Nothing is generated or invented — every result is queried live from OpenStreetMap, Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata.
